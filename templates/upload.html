How to import trades

  • 1. Open Tradovate (web) and go to "Reports" on the right hand side menu.
  • 2. Select the account you want to export from in the drop list on the left.
  • 3. From the [Performance] tab, select the dates and download CSV.
  • 4. Drop your CSV file here.
  • 5. Make sure the right account is selected on the top left.
  • 6. Click "Preview fills" — each row is one partial fill.
  • 7. Select fills and click "Group into trade" to build your trades.
  • 8. Click "Import trades" when all fills are grouped.


Fees configured in settings will be applied per fill.
Ungrouped fills will not be imported.
